# Weapon damage and role balance

**Status: every value here is pending playtest.** The tables are model output, not measurements. Tune the
override files below after LAN sessions; the role invariants keep a change from silently breaking a role.

## Override layer

Weapon definitions are built in layers, lowest first (`WeaponLayers`):

1. generated class placeholders (`WeaponPlaceholders`, shaped by `WeaponRole`);
2. datapack definitions `data/<namespace>/weapons/<id>.json`;
3. the local CS2 export `~/.csmc/cache/data/weapons.vdata` (`Cs2WeaponImporter`);
4. **balance overrides** `data/<namespace>/weapon_balance/<id>.json` (`WeaponBalance`).

The balance layer is last, so exported vdata can no longer silently win over authored balance. It is rebuilt
on startup and on every `/reload`. A file targets the weapon with the same id and may contain only:

- numbers: `damage`, `headshot_multiplier`, `armor_ratio`, `armor_bonus`, `range`, `range_modifier`,
  `penetration_power`, `penetration_count` (integer), `penetration_distance`, `cycle_time_s`, `spread_base`,
  `magazine_size` (integer), `reload_time_s`, `max_speed`;
- `inaccuracy` `{stand, crouch, move, jump, land, ladder, fire}`;
- `recoil` `{angle, angle_variance, magnitude, magnitude_variance}`;
- `scoped` `{spread_base, max_speed, cycle_time_s, inaccuracy{…}}`;
- keys starting with `_` are comments (the bundled files keep their reason in `_reason`).

Files are strict JSON holding exactly one object: broken syntax, comments or trailing content after the object
reject the file. Nested objects merge member by member. A file with any other member, a non-numeric value, no
matching weapon, or a result that fails weapon validation is logged (`Ignoring weapon balance override …`) and
ignored as a whole; the weapon keeps its pre-override definition and the reload still succeeds. Only deviations are
stored: the bundled files never restate CS2 numbers, which keep coming from the user's own export.

Both unscoped and scoped `max_speed` must be finite, positive and at most 2,000 units/s, matching the
movement-state protocol's held-speed bound. An over-limit override is rejected as a whole through the same
invalid-override path.

because without a scope they otherwise cannot fill their long-range roles.

Kept unchanged, with reasons:

- `Ballistics.CS_TO_MC_HEALTH` = 0.2: 100 CS HP maps to 20 Minecraft health, so CS kill counts carry over
  and every gun already out-damages a netherite sword (8 per 0.625 s) at its role range.
- Knife (`data/csmc/knives/*.json`): the default knife's first slash is 40 CS (8 health) with a 0.5 s cooldown
  (follow-ups 25 CS every 0.4 s); the other knives slash 30–40 CS every 0.32–0.5 s. That is about 12.5–16
  health/s, comparable to an unenchanted netherite sword without criticals (12.8/s). Knife damage is reduced by
  chest armor (armor ratio 1.7: 85 % gets through kevlar); the 180 CS stab backstab kills through any unenchanted
  armor (Protection enchantments still apply after it).
- Grenades: HE 99 CS peak is weapons.vdata's `m_nDamage`; the 6-block radius and the fire values (16 CS per
  0.5 s) are authored (`GrenadeTuning`), not CS2 data. HE damage is a vanilla explosion, so vanilla armor and
  Blast Protection reduce it. C4 (500 CS peak, 8-block radius) stays far above HE; no incoherence found.

## Model

`BalanceModel` (unit tests, pure math) per hit: range falloff `rangeModifier^(units/500)` at 16 units per
block, hit group (head = weapon multiplier, body = chest 1.0), `ArmorModel.healthDamage` with the tier
protection (iron 0.5, diamond 0.65, netherite 0.8, kevlar 1.0), then `CS_TO_MC_HEALTH`. Accuracy is the
expected share of pellets inside an aim circle (body radius 0.3, head 0.25 blocks), using the same two-disc
spread as `Ballistics.applySpread`, standing still, stand inaccuracy plus the fire penalty decaying over the
recovery time. Weapons with a scope use it at medium and long range and hip-fire up close.

Firing is simulated tick by tick as the server does it (`GunSystem.tick`, `ShooterState`,
`Ballistics.fire`): at least one tick between shots and fractional cycle deadlines; the spray index restarts after
more than `ceil(cycle × 20) + 4` quiet ticks; the fire penalty decays every tick, also during reloads; a reload
starts the tick after the magazine empties, lasts `max(1, ceil(reload × 20))` ticks and overlaps the cooldown.
Reloading clears the scope and any pending bolt rezoom and zoom is rejected while reloading, so scoped fire
resumes one tick after the reload finishes (the zoom transition time is client presentation only). The
shooter may wait 0–30 ticks between shots, whichever kills soonest (spray close, tap far); recoil is assumed
compensated. TTK is the expected first-passage time over random hits (first shot at 0 s); shots are expected
shots. `BalanceModelTest` pins these rules with hand-computed fixtures. Bands: close 4, medium 16, long 48 blocks.
"Crafts/kill" = expected rounds per zombie kill ÷ recipe yield (pistol/rifle 16, sniper/shotgun 8).

Endermen take bullet damage: `EndermanBulletMixin` exempts `csmc:bullet` from their projectile dodge
(covered by `ArmorGameTests`).

Role invariants (`BalanceInvariantTest`, on the no-cache defaults and, when present, the local export):

- snipers need one hit on an unarmored player or zombie, head and body, at long range; only the AWP one-shots
  a kevlar body;
- the SMG median TTK beats the rifle median up close (player, zombie, skeleton);
- the shotgun median is the fastest up close against every player armor tier (ties allowed only unarmored)
  and the slowest at long range;
- every gun kills a zombie at its role range faster than a netherite sword (1.25 s);
- no gun is best or tied in every band against both unarmored and kevlar players;
- kevlar never lowers TTK, is never weaker than netherite, and raises hits-to-kill by ≥ 25 % on average at
  medium range.

Regenerate the tables: `CSMC_WRITE_BALANCE_DOC=1 ./gradlew test --tests dev.csmc.balance.BalanceInvariantTest`.
Cells read `shots/TTK s`; band cells are close · medium · long.
